Nail Care Pro Tips

01

Moisturize Daily

Keep cuticles and nails hydrated with cuticle oil or hand cream to prevent dryness and cracking.

02

File Correctly

File in one direction only to prevent splitting and weakening. Never use a sawing motion back and forth.

03

Base Coat Always

Always apply a base coat before polish to protect nails from staining and help polish last longer.

04

Let Nails Breathe

Give your nails a break from polish occasionally to prevent yellowing and allow them to strengthen.
