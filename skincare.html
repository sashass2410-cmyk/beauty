Skincare Pro Tips

01

Cleanse Twice Daily

Wash your face morning and night to remove dirt, oil, and makeup. Always use lukewarm water, never hot.

02

Layer Products Correctly

Apply products from thinnest to thickest consistency: toner, serum, moisturizer, then SPF in the morning.

03

Never Skip SPF

Wear sunscreen every day, even indoors and on cloudy days. It's the most important anti-aging step.

04

Stay Hydrated

Drink plenty of water throughout the day. Internal hydration is just as important as topical moisturizers.
