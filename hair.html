Hair Care Pro Tips

01

Wash Less Often

Over-washing can strip natural oils. Try washing 2-3 times per week and use dry shampoo between washes.

02

Cold Water Rinse

Rinse with cold water to seal the hair cuticle, add shine, and reduce frizz for smoother, healthier-looking hair.

03

Regular Trims

Get regular trims every 6-8 weeks to prevent split ends and maintain healthy hair growth.

04

Sleep on Silk

Use a silk or satin pillowcase to reduce friction, prevent breakage, and wake up with smoother hair.
