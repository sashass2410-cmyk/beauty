Pro Makeup Tips

01

Always Prime First

Use a primer to create a smooth canvas and help your makeup last longer throughout the day.

02

Blend, Blend, Blend

The key to professional-looking makeup is seamless blending. Invest in good quality brushes and sponges.

03

Match Your Foundation

Always test foundation on your jawline in natural light to ensure a perfect match with your skin tone.

04

Set Your Makeup

Lock in your look with a setting spray or powder to ensure your makeup stays fresh all day.
